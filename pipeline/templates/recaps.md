# Stage: recaps ("Story so far")

**Output:** `data-src/<book>/recaps.json`. See "recaps.json" in `docs/data-format.md`.

- Write a checkpoint every 10–15% of the book, at natural breaks (the end of a part or a turning point). A 47-chapter book gets about 4–6.
- Each has a `through` chapter, a short `title`, and 2–5 paragraphs covering chapters 1 to `through` as a whole story, not chapter by chapter.
- Use only what's known by `through`, and respect `reveals.json`.
