# Stage: glossary

**Output:** `data-src/<book>/notes-<a>-<b>.json` for your range. See "notes-*.json" in `docs/data-format.md`.

Explain what a modern reader might not know: old words, titles and ranks, money (with a sense of what it's worth), history, places, customs, foreign phrases, references (Bible, myth, literature), and the book's own lore.

- Write 2–4 sentences each, specific to how the book uses the term.
- Give 3–10 notes per chapter depending on density.
- `chapter` is where the term first appears.
- `quote` (a few words from the text) is for text mode, public-domain books only.
- A note must not explain a term's later significance.
