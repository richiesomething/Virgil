# Identity decisions (spoiler gating)

Chapter `characters` lists and notes use these ids. Where a character is a disguise or secret
identity, the app keeps them separate until the reader can know, then folds them together with
`mergedInto` in `characters.json`.

| Who | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Sinbad the Sailor, the Count of Monte Cristo, the English clerk of Thomson & French, Lord Wilmore, Abbé Busoni | Always `edmond-dantes`. The text makes each link clear to the reader as it is introduced: Lord Wilmore is his passport name in ch 25; Sinbad and the Englishman in ch 30; Franz identifies the Count as Sinbad in ch 34; Busoni is named in ch 43, by which point the reader knows the abbé of ch 26–27 was Dantès. Each appears as an alias with its chapter. |
| Count de Morcerf | Always `fernand-mondego`; Caderousse tells the reader in ch 27. |
| Haydée | `greek-girl` ("the Count's Greek companion") in ch 34–44, before she is named; she is named in ch 45, where `greek-girl` merges into `haydee`. |
| Benedetto / Andrea Cavalcanti | `benedetto` is the foundling of Bertuccio's story (ch 44–45). `andrea-cavalcanti` is introduced in ch 54. Bertuccio recognizes Andrea as Benedetto in ch 62, where `benedetto` merges into `andrea-cavalcanti`. |
