# Virgil roadmap

Guiding rule: **no spoilers**. Everything is gated by the reader's bookmark.

## Phase 1 — Core (done)
1. Library / book picker
2. Bookmark and progress
3. Chapter summaries (short and long)
4. Character list up to the current chapter, with "new this chapter"
5. Spoiler-aware character profiles

## Phase 2 — Relationships and structure
6. ✅ Character map (relationship graph, click a character for their relationships and profile, replay slider that never passes the bookmark, filters by group/importance/relationship type; profiles list relationships too)
7. ◐ Family trees: a "Family only" view on the map (generations in rows, spouses side by side) is done; a dedicated tree is worth building when a multi-generation saga is added
8. ✅ Places and settings (also important objects), on the map and with their own profiles
9. Timeline of events (story order vs. telling order)
10. Plot threads / open questions

## Phase 3 — Help with difficult books
11. ✅ Name decoder — search finds anyone by nickname, title or disguise (only once revealed)
12. ✅ Glossary of archaic and period terms, money and measures (chapter notes + growing glossary)
13. ✅ Translations of foreign-language passages (glossary "foreign" notes)
14. ✅ Allusions explained (glossary "reference" notes)
15. ✅ Historical and cultural context per chapter (glossary "history"/"custom" notes)
16. Themes and motifs tracker
17. ✅ "Previously on…" recap after a break (welcome back + Story so far), plus "Before you read" primers
18. ✅ Quick lookup: search from anywhere

## Phase 4 — Personal and social
19. Notes, highlights, saved quotes
20. Ask Virgil: chat that only knows the book up to your bookmark
21. Discussion questions per chapter
22. ✅ Reading plan: goals, projected finish, stats and streaks
23. Book club mode
24. Study mode (quizzes, key passages; unlocked when finished)

## Later
- Matching chapters across editions and translations
- Syncing reading position from e-readers or audiobooks
- Audio pronunciation of names
- Spoiler-free "before you start" primers
